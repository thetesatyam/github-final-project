# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, nationality, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people
- Respecting different opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback
- Taking responsibility for mistakes and learning from them
- Focusing on what is best for the community

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwelcome sexual attention
- Trolling, insulting, derogatory, or personal attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing another person's private information without permission
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing the standards of acceptable behavior. They are expected to take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to behavior that is inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces and also applies when someone is officially representing the project or community in public spaces, events, communications, or other activities.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project team. Reports should be reviewed and investigated, with appropriate action taken based on the circumstances. The privacy of the person reporting an incident should be respected.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Community leaders may use the following actions depending on the seriousness and circumstances of a violation:

1. **Correction** – A private or public clarification explaining the inappropriate behavior and why it is unacceptable.
2. **Warning** – A formal warning when inappropriate behavior continues or is more serious.
3. **Temporary Ban** – Temporary removal from community participation when necessary.
4. **Permanent Ban** – Permanent removal when severe or repeated violations occur.
